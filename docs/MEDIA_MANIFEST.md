## Selection and playback

The official homepage gallery was reviewed. Three photos and the exact header SVG fill useful gaps; other gallery images did not displace stronger supplied media. Existing official product cutouts remain in the hero and matching product cards. Category photos may represent several recipes and are labeled in details. No generic bottle illustrations or AI product imagery were introduced.

The Tropical Bowl video is much larger than its supporting still. Sweet Protein uses an asymmetric editorial composition. Other footage remains available for future pages without autoplaying on Home. Local posters and lazy video sources keep initial loading light. Only one visible video plays at once on mobile; offscreen footage and background footage behind dialogs pause. Reduced motion, Save-Data and pause controls are respected.

The original JPEG logo remains historical media only. The active logo is the exact official header SVG, not a recreated wordmark.
