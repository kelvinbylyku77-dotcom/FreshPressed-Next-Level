# Fresh Pressed media catalog

All locally bundled video files in `site/public/media/` are optimized web-ready MP4 files (vertical 540x960 or equivalent web-friendly proxies, muted/no-audio) so the handoff stays small enough to upload. Poster frames live in `site/public/media/posters/`. User-supplied stills and selected existing stills are also in `site/public/media/`.

- `green-drink-30fps.mp4` — strong Fresh Pressed branded green drink, good color and product focus.

## Important creative rule
Do not place every video on the homepage. Curate the best 8–12 assets and use the rest on Shop, collection, PDP, About/Story, or future campaign pages. Too much autoplay video will make the site feel busy and slow.
